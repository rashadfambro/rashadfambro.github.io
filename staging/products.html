PackageDesk

Package & mail-order intake for front desks and mail rooms
Setup $2,000 · plans from $90/mo

Built for: Apartment communities, hotels, resorts, and timeshares that receive packages for residents, guests, and owners.

How It Works

  1. A carrier drops off a delivery; staff snap the label or scan it, and PackageDesk reads the name, unit or reservation, carrier, and tracking number.
  2. It matches the package to the right resident or guest — or to an upcoming reservation for orders shipped ahead of arrival — and staff confirm the match.
  3. The recipient gets a text or email with a one-time pickup code and where to collect it; perishables and signature items are flagged first.
  4. At pickup, staff enter the code and capture a signature or photo, so every hand-off has an audit trail.
  5. Unclaimed items get automatic reminders, then a return-to-sender or disposal step your policy defines; managers see daily volume and aging.
Saves MoneyFewer front-desk minutes per package, fewer lost-package claims, and no paper logbooks to search.
Wins More BusinessResidents and guests get their deliveries faster, which shows up in reviews and renewals; guest mail orders are waiting at check-in.
Boosts ProductivityStaff log a package in seconds instead of minutes and stop fielding "did my package come?" calls.
Human in the LoopStaff confirm every match before anyone is notified, and release only with the pickup code or an ID check.
