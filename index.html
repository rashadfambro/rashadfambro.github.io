    11 Questions
    
    
    Common Questions
    
    
    
      
    Do I Need to Be “Techy” to Work With You?
    No. I explain everything in plain language and set things up so your staff can use them with a short training session. You focus on running the business.
    
      
    Will I Have to Replace the Software I Already Use?
    Usually not. I start with the tools you already pay for and connect or extend them. I only recommend new software when it clearly saves more than it costs.
    
      
    How Do You Use AI?
    Only where it helps, and always with four rules: a person approves anything that matters (prices, promises, refunds, legal or medical answers); only the data needed for the job is used; tools are set up in your business’s own accounts, so you keep ownership; and every change is tested with your team first, with a manual fallback.
    
      
    How Much Does a Project Cost?
    Desk product setups run $500–$3,500, with fully managed monthly plans from $80. The AI Readiness Assessment is $750 (virtual), custom pilots start at $3,500, and the Care & Growth Plan starts at $300/month. Every build gets a fixed, written quote first. Founding clients get 25% off their first setup or project and their first month free.
    
      
    How Long Until We See Results?
    Most pilots launch in 2–6 weeks. We agree on a starting measure up front, like response time or staff minutes per request, so you can see the difference.
    
      
    Is AI Safe for My Customers’ Information?
    We decide together what data the tools can see. Sensitive actions require staff approval, and I keep private information out of AI tools unless you’ve approved how it’s handled. For regulated businesses like dental or legal offices, we review your obligations before anything is built.
    
      
    Do You Work With Businesses Outside Hospitality and Property?
    Yes — salons, barbershops, spas, restaurants, contractors, real estate, law and dental offices, retail, and more. If your team repeats the same tasks every day, there’s likely something worth automating.
    
      
    Do You Meet Virtually or in Person?
    Both. Most work happens over video calls with clients worldwide. For on-site assessments, audits, or launch training, I’m happy to travel to your business — travel expenses (airfare, lodging, mileage) are billed at cost, approved by you in advance, and added to the assessment or project rate.
    
      
    Will the Tools Carry My Brand?
    Yes. Every tool is branded for your business — your logo, colors, domain, and voice. Tools are delivered as a managed service: updates go out through a tested, versioned release process with release notes, staged rollouts, and the ability to roll back.
    
      
    Why Keep a Human in the Loop?
    AI is fast but not perfect. Having staff approve prices, promises, and sensitive replies protects your customers and your reputation, catches mistakes before they go out, and keeps your team in charge. Routine, low-risk steps like reminders can run automatically.
    
      
    Is There a Free Trial?
    Yes. Every tool includes a free 14-day trial with a license key — no payment details needed. At the end, choose a plan or let it expire; nothing is charged automatically.
    
      
    How Much Are the Monthly Plans?
    Standard tools are $80, $150, or $250 per month (Essentials, Growth, Pro). Property and hospitality tools are $150, $250, or $400 per property per month. Any 3 standard tools on Essentials are $200/month. Every plan is fully managed: hosting, updates, monitoring, testing, and support are included. Text-message and phone-number fees are passed through at cost.
    
      
    Can I Pay Over Time?
    Yes. Any setup or project of $1,000 or more can be split into 3 interest-free monthly payments, with the first payment at signing. You can pay online with PayPal or Square; PayPal may also offer its own Pay Later options at checkout.
    
      
    Can I Buy Directly on the Site?
    Yes. Tap Buy on any product or package, choose pay in full or a payment plan, and check out securely with PayPal or Square. Custom builds are quoted first; you can pay the deposit online once you accept the proposal.
    
      
    What Happens After Launch?
    You get a 30-day results check included, plus the option of a monthly care plan for support, updates, and new improvements.
